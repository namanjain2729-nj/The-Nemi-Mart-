1. Nature of Relationship

Aap ek Independent Direct Seller hain. Aap YTM India Marketing Pvt. Ltd. ya The Nemi Mart ke employee, partner ya franchisee nahi hain. Aap apne business ke khud malik hain aur apni working hours ke liye zimmedar hain.

2. Fixed Sales Commission (SC) Policy

Jab tak The Nemi Mart ki taraf se koi naya written update ya modification jari nahi kiya jata, tab tak nimnalikhit fixed SC (Sales Commission) hi sabhi independent sellers ko di jayegi:

  • MRP below ₹250 → ₹25 SC
  • MRP ₹250–₹500 → ₹50 SC
  • MRP ₹501–₹1,000 → ₹100 SC
  • MRP ₹1,001–₹1,500 → ₹150 SC
  • MRP above ₹1,500 → ₹200 SC

3. Mandatory Payment & QR Code Rule (Strict)

Sabhi sellers ke liye anivarya niyam: Customer se payment hamesha The Nemi Mart ke official designated UPI ID / QR Code par hi liya jayega. Koi bhi independent seller apna personal ya kisi anya vyakti ka QR code/UPI use nahi karega. Daily total number of products aur approved SC ke anusar kul rashi (Total Amount) agle 24 ghante ke bheetar seller ke registered official account/UPI mein bhej di jayegi.

4. Direct Selling & Consumer Protection Compliance

Yeh business Consumer Protection Act, 2019 aur Consumer Protection (Direct Selling) Rules, 2021 ke niyamoh ke antargat chalta hai. Kisi bhi tarah ka pyramid scheme, money-circulation, ya false/guaranteed income promise karna poori tarah ban hai. Earnings kewal genuine product sales par hi depend karti hain.

5. Product Authenticity & Prohibited Conduct

Products original aur lawfully sourced hone chahiye. Labels, batch numbers, expiry dates ya invoices ke sath koi chhedkhani nahi ki jayegi. Jhoothe medical, health ya guaranteed profit claims banana sakht manah hai.

6. Termination & Safety

Agar koi seller fraud, tampered products, unauthorized representation ya rules todne mein shamil paya gaya, toh The Nemi Mart uska account turant suspend ya terminate kar sakta hai.
